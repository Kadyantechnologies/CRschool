Our Vision

To be a center of excellence in education that empowers students to be compassionate, innovative, and responsible leaders of tomorrow, equipped to face global challenges with integrity and resilience.

Our Mission

To provide a holistic learning environment that fosters academic rigor, critical thinking, physical well-being, and moral values, ensuring every student realizes their full potential in a safe, inclusive setting.

Our Roots

A Legacy Since 1995

Established in 1995, CR Sr. Sec. School began with a humble vision to bring quality education to Majra (Dubaldhan). Over the past three decades, we have grown from a small community school into a premier educational institution recognized across the region.

Our journey is marked by consistently excellent academic results, state-level sports achievements, and a growing alumni network of successful professionals. The school's infrastructure has continually evolved, incorporating smart classrooms, advanced laboratories, and expansive sports facilities to keep pace with modern educational demands.
