Academics & Curriculum

Our Curriculum

Stages of Learning

We follow a progressive curriculum designed to seamlessly transition students from early childhood education to senior secondary specialization.

Primary School

Our primary curriculum focuses on foundational literacy and numeracy. We use play-way and interactive methods to make learning enjoyable. Subjects include English, Hindi, Mathematics, Environmental Studies, and basic IT skills.

Middle School

In middle school, the focus shifts to conceptual understanding and analytical skills. Students delve deeper into Sciences, Social Sciences, Mathematics, and Languages. Project-based learning becomes a core component.

Senior Secondary

We offer specialized streams (Science, Arts) to help students prepare for their career paths and higher education. Our rigorous academic training is supplemented by comprehensive career counseling.

Pedagogy

Teaching Methodology

Our teaching methodology goes beyond rote memorization. We employ a blend of traditional wisdom and modern educational practices to ensure a holistic learning experience.

  • Experiential Learning: Hands-on projects and lab experiments.
  • Technology Integration: Smart boards and digital resources in every classroom.
  • Personalized Attention: Favorable student-teacher ratio to cater to individual needs.
  • Continuous Assessment: Regular evaluations to track progress and provide feedback.
